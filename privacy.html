Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

4/4: Simple Metronome does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Your settings (such as tempo and time signature) are stored only on your device and never leave it.

Because we collect no data, there is nothing to request, export, or delete on our end. Removing the app removes any settings it stored.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at semple.studio@hey.com.
